# 🌿 Nouriva — Nourishing Life Through Intelligent Food Choices

**Nouriva** is an AI-powered food and nutrition project designed to help people make smarter food choices, understand their food habits, and plan meals according to their health needs and budget.

Our goal is simple: make healthy eating easier, smarter, and more accessible for everyone. 💚

## ✨ Our Features

### 🥗 Feature 1: AI Food & Health Scanner

Explore food and nutrition information with an AI-powered scanning feature to make more informed food choices.

### 🧠 Feature 2: Personalized AI Nutrition Coach

Get personalized nutrition guidance designed around individual needs and goals to support healthier eating habits.

### 🍱 Feature 3: Budget-Aware AI Meal Planner

Plan meals with your budget in mind and discover practical ways to make nutritious food choices more affordable.

### 📊 Feature 4: Nutrition Tracker & Food Assistant

Track food and nutrition information and get assistance with everyday food choices to support better eating habits.

### 🖥️ Main Dashboard

A central interface that brings Nouriva's features together in one place, creating a more consistent and user-friendly experience.

## 🎯 Our Mission

We believe healthy eating should not be confusing or expensive.

Nouriva combines artificial intelligence, nutrition awareness, and budget-friendly planning to help people build better eating habits, one meal at a time.
